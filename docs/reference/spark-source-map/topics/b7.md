## What it is

A join in Spark is two decisions, made at different times by different code. The **join type** (inner, left, semi, anti…) is yours and is fixed at analysis. The **join strategy** — how the rows are physically brought together — is Spark's, decided during physical planning by `JoinSelection`, and possibly *revised again at runtime* by AQE.

There are five physical strategies, and the whole performance story of joins is which one you get:

| Strategy | Shuffle? | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| `BroadcastHashJoinExec` | no — one side broadcast | one side under the broadcast threshold |
| `ShuffledHashJoinExec` | yes | one side small enough to build a local hash map |
| `SortMergeJoinExec` | yes, plus sort | equi-join keys that are orderable |
| `BroadcastNestedLoopJoinExec` | no | anything — the non-equi fallback |
| `CartesianProductExec` | yes | inner join with no condition |

The last two are the ones that turn a query from slow into never-finishing, and both are chosen silently when nothing better applies.

## Code path

```
df1.join(df2, on="id", how="inner")

--- analysis ---
  → Join logical node; DetectAmbiguousSelfJoin      [analysis/DetectAmbiguousSelfJoin.scala]
        guarded by spark.sql.analyzer.failAmbiguousSelfJoin

--- optimizer ---
  → predicate pushdown / column pruning through the join
  → CBO join reorder (off by default)   spark.sql.cbo.joinReorder.enabled

--- physical planning: the strategy decision ---
  → JoinSelection                        [SparkStrategies.scala:181]
      hint path first                    [SparkStrategies.scala:198-199, 324]
      then, in strict priority order     [SparkStrategies.scala:306-308]
        createBroadcastHashJoin(false)   [:241]  ← canBroadcastBySize / getBroadcastBuildSide
        createShuffleHashJoin(false)     [:261]  ← blocked by preferSortMergeJoin=true
        createSortMergeJoin()            [:286]  ← the usual answer
      no equi-keys → BroadcastNestedLoopJoinExec, or CartesianProductExec

  → JoinSelectionHelper                  [optimizer/joins.scala]
        getBroadcastBuildSide      [:292]
        getShuffleHashJoinBuildSide[:318]   consults preferSortMergeJoin [:327,:337]
        canBroadcastBySize         [:367]   plan.stats.sizeInBytes <= threshold
        canBuildBroadcastLeft/Right[:377,:384]  join type restricts which side

--- exchange insertion ---
  → EnsureRequirements                   [exchange/EnsureRequirements.scala:51]
        reads each operator's requiredChildDistribution
        SortMergeJoin/ShuffledHashJoin   [joins/ShuffledJoin.scala:37]  → HashPartitioning
        BroadcastHashJoin                [joins/BroadcastHashJoinExec.scala:67] → BroadcastDistribution

--- execution ---
  BroadcastHashJoinExec [joins/BroadcastHashJoinExec.scala:40]
      build side → HashedRelation [joins/HashedRelation.scala:136] → broadcast to executors
      driver collects the build side first: spark.sql.broadcastTimeout applies here
  SortMergeJoinExec     [joins/SortMergeJoinExec.scala:39]
  ShuffledHashJoinExec  [joins/ShuffledHashJoinExec.scala:38]

--- runtime revision (AQE) ---
  → DynamicJoinSelection  [adaptive/DynamicJoinSelection.scala:38]
        real shuffle sizes may promote a join to broadcast
  → OptimizeSkewedJoin    [adaptive/OptimizeSkewedJoin.scala:57], optimizeSkewJoin [:222]
        splits partitions above skewedPartitionFactor × median AND the byte threshold
```

### Strategy selection — the decision that matters

- [SparkStrategies.scala:181](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/SparkStrategies.scala#L181) — `object JoinSelection`: the single place a join strategy is chosen
- [SparkStrategies.scala:306](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/SparkStrategies.scala#L306) — the priority chain, readable in three lines: `createBroadcastHashJoin(false).orElse(createShuffleHashJoin(false)).orElse(createSortMergeJoin())`. Everything about join tuning is about moving your query up this list
- [SparkStrategies.scala:324](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/SparkStrategies.scala#L324) — the **hint** path runs first with `onlyLookingAtHint = true`; an invalid build side in a hint raises rather than being ignored (L198–L199)
- [optimizer/joins.scala:367](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/catalyst/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/catalyst/optimizer/joins.scala#L367) — `canBroadcastBySize`: compares `plan.stats.sizeInBytes` against the threshold. This is an **estimate from statistics**, not a measurement — which is why a broadcast can be chosen for something that turns out not to fit
- [optimizer/joins.scala:377](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/catalyst/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/catalyst/optimizer/joins.scala#L377) — `canBuildBroadcastLeft` / `canBuildBroadcastRight` (L384): the join *type* constrains which side may be built. A left outer join can only broadcast the right side — so a `BROADCAST` hint on the wrong side is silently unusable
- [optimizer/joins.scala:318](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/catalyst/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/catalyst/optimizer/joins.scala#L318) — `getShuffleHashJoinBuildSide`: gated on `!conf.preferSortMergeJoin` (L327, L337). Since `spark.sql.join.preferSortMergeJoin` defaults to **true**, shuffled hash join is effectively off by default — a strategy most people never see

### The five operators

- [joins/BroadcastHashJoinExec.scala:40](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/BroadcastHashJoinExec.scala#L40) — no shuffle; `requiredChildDistribution` (L67) asks for `BroadcastDistribution`
- [joins/SortMergeJoinExec.scala:39](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/SortMergeJoinExec.scala#L39) — the default for large-large equi-joins
- [joins/ShuffledHashJoinExec.scala:38](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/ShuffledHashJoinExec.scala#L38) — shuffle without sort
- [joins/BroadcastNestedLoopJoinExec.scala:34](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/BroadcastNestedLoopJoinExec.scala#L34) — **the non-equi fallback**. A join condition with `>` or `!=` instead of `=` lands here, and it is O(n×m). The most common cause of a join that never finishes
- [joins/CartesianProductExec.scala:67](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/CartesianProductExec.scala#L67) — inner join with no condition at all. Note `spark.sql.crossJoin.enabled` defaults to **`true`** in 4.x, so Spark no longer refuses to run one
- [joins/ShuffledJoin.scala:37](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/ShuffledJoin.scala#L37) — the shared `requiredChildDistribution` for both shuffle-based joins: `HashPartitioning` on the join keys, which is what forces the exchange

### Build-side construction and exchange

- [joins/HashedRelation.scala:136](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/joins/HashedRelation.scala#L136) — `HashedRelation.apply`: builds `UnsafeHashedRelation` (L209) or the specialised `LongHashedRelation` when the key is a single long. This object is what gets broadcast
- [exchange/EnsureRequirements.scala:51](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/exchange/EnsureRequirements.scala#L51) — inserts the exchanges by reading each operator's `requiredChildDistribution` (L59). The shuffle in a join is not part of the join operator — it is a separate node added here, which is why `Exchange` and `SortMergeJoin` appear as siblings in an `EXPLAIN`

### Runtime revision — AQE re-decides

- [adaptive/DynamicJoinSelection.scala:38](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/adaptive/DynamicJoinSelection.scala#L38) — after a shuffle completes, real sizes are known and a sort-merge join can be promoted to a broadcast. Governed by `spark.sql.adaptive.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold`, which is *separate* from the static threshold
- [adaptive/OptimizeSkewedJoin.scala:57](https://github.com/apache/spark/blob/v4.2.0/sql/core/src/main/scala/org/apache/spark/sql/execution/adaptive/OptimizeSkewedJoin.scala#L57) — skew handling; `optimizeSkewJoin` at L222. A partition is skewed only when it exceeds **both** `skewedPartitionFactor × median` **and** `skewedPartitionThresholdInBytes` (256MB) — so a skewed-but-small partition is deliberately left alone

## Relevant configs

| Config | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `spark.sql.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold` | `10MB` | Static broadcast cutoff, compared against *estimated* size; `-1` disables broadcast joins |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold` | (none → falls back to static) | Separate AQE threshold applied after real sizes are known |
| `spark.sql.broadcastTimeout` | `300s` | Time limit for collecting and broadcasting the build side; a common failure on a marginal broadcast |
| `spark.sql.join.preferSortMergeJoin` | `true` | Effectively disables shuffled hash join — set `false` to make it reachable |
| `spark.sql.crossJoin.enabled` | `true` | Cartesian products are permitted by default in 4.x |
| `spark.sql.analyzer.failAmbiguousSelfJoin` | `true` | Fail rather than guess when a self-join column reference is ambiguous |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.skewJoin.enabled` | `true` | AQE splits skewed partitions |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.skewJoin.skewedPartitionFactor` | `5.0` | A partition must exceed 5× the median… |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.skewJoin.skewedPartitionThresholdInBytes` | `256MB` | …**and** this size, before it counts as skewed |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.forceOptimizeSkewedJoin` | `false` | Apply skew handling even when it adds an extra shuffle |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.nonEmptyPartitionRatioForBroadcastJoin` | `0.2` | Blocks AQE broadcast promotion when too many partitions are non-empty |
| `spark.sql.adaptive.maxShuffledHashJoinLocalMapThreshold` | `0` | Per-partition size allowing AQE to pick a local shuffled hash join |
| `spark.sql.shuffle.partitions` | `200` | Partition count for the join's exchange |
| `spark.sql.cbo.joinReorder.enabled` | `false` | Cost-based multi-way join reordering — off by default, needs `ANALYZE TABLE` statistics |
| `spark.sql.cbo.joinReorder.dp.threshold` | `12` | Max tables considered by the DP reorder search |
| `spark.sql.bucketing.coalesceBucketsInJoin.enabled` | `false` | Coalesce mismatched bucket counts instead of shuffling |
| `spark.sql.cartesianProductExec.buffer.in.memory.threshold` | `4096` | Rows buffered in memory per cartesian partition before spilling |
| `spark.sql.execution.broadcastHashJoin.outputPartitioningExpandLimit` | `8` | Cap on expanding a broadcast join's output partitioning |

## Chapter notes

Gap analysis against **spark-book Ch 10** (`spark-book/ch10-joins.md`).

### Already in spark-book ✅

- All seven join types with semantics, including `left_semi` and `left_anti`
- Equi-join shorthand and the `on=` forms
- Column-clash handling and disambiguation
- The `BROADCAST` hint and when a broadcast join is appropriate

### Not in spark-book — needs addition ❌

- **The strategy priority chain is three lines of code and explains all join tuning.** `broadcast → shuffled hash → sort-merge`, tried in that order. Every piece of join advice is about moving a query up this list. The chapter discusses broadcast joins without presenting the ladder they sit at the top of.
- **`preferSortMergeJoin=true` means shuffled hash join is effectively off.** `getShuffleHashJoinBuildSide` is gated on the negation of that config, so the middle rung of the ladder is unreachable by default. Practitioners routinely do not know this strategy exists.
- **Join *type* restricts which side can be broadcast.** `canBuildBroadcastLeft`/`Right` mean a left outer join can only broadcast its right side. A `BROADCAST` hint naming the wrong side simply does not apply — which looks like the hint being ignored.
- **`canBroadcastBySize` uses an estimate, not a measurement.** It reads `plan.stats.sizeInBytes`. Bad or missing statistics are why a broadcast is sometimes chosen for something that then blows up the driver or trips `spark.sql.broadcastTimeout`.
- **Non-equi joins fall to `BroadcastNestedLoopJoinExec`, which is O(n×m).** A condition using `>` or `!=` rather than `=` silently leaves the hash-join world entirely. This is the single most common cause of a join that appears to hang, and the chapter does not mention it.
- **`spark.sql.crossJoin.enabled` defaults to `true` in 4.x.** Spark no longer refuses an accidental cartesian product; the old guardrail people remember is off.
- **The shuffle is not part of the join operator.** `EnsureRequirements` inserts `Exchange` nodes based on each operator's `requiredChildDistribution`. Knowing this makes an `EXPLAIN` legible — the `Exchange` above a `SortMergeJoin` is *why* the join is expensive, and is a separate node.
- **AQE re-decides the strategy after the shuffle.** `DynamicJoinSelection` can promote a planned sort-merge join to a broadcast once real sizes are known, using a *separate* threshold (`spark.sql.adaptive.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold`). This is why the plan in the SQL tab may not match `explain()` output — connects directly to the B3 finding about pre-AQE plans.
- **Skew detection requires two conditions, not one.** A partition must exceed both 5× the median *and* 256MB. A skewed-but-small partition is intentionally ignored, which explains "AQE didn't fix my skew".
- **`NEAREST BY` exists as of 4.2.0.** A top-K ranking join with its own logical node, with an `approx` flag reserved for future indexed ANN strategies.
